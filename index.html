HOW IT WORKS

Three steps. Fully automatic.

Connect once, then let the system handle every failed payment from that point forward.

STEP 01

Connect Stripe

OAuth connect in under 2 minutes. We listen to your webhook events — no code, no dev time, no disruption to existing billing.

STEP 02

Classify Every Failure

We classify the decline code, customer history, card type, and billing pattern. Insufficient funds gets a different strategy than an expired card.

STEP 03

Smart Recovery Runs

Retries fire at the optimal window. Nudges go out via the best available channel. Dashboard updates in real time. You get paid.

WHY REFLOW WINS
