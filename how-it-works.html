THE FLOW

What happens the moment a payment fails

Step 01

💳 Payment Fails

A customer's charge is declined by their bank or card issuer. Your payment processor fires a webhook event. Reflow receives it within milliseconds and begins the recovery process.

Step 02

🔍 Classify the Failure

Reflow decodes the decline code, customer payment history, card type, and billing pattern. It picks from 14 failure categories — each gets a distinct recovery playbook.

Step 03

⏰ Pick the Right Moment to Retry

For insufficient funds, we check historical payday patterns and retry on Wednesday at 9am — when the customer's salary likely hit. For expired cards, we nudge immediately and retry on update. Timing is everything.

Step 04

📨 Nudge Through the Right Channel

If a retry alone won't work (e.g., expired card), Reflow sends a recovery nudge through email, SMS, or in-app — depending on what that customer responds to. Not spam. Targeted, timed messaging.

✅ Revenue Recovered. You Pay Nothing Until Then.

The retry succeeds. Your payment processor confirms the charge. Your dashboard updates in real time. Reflow invoices you 15% of the recovered amount at end of month. If nothing is recovered, you owe nothing.

FAILURE TYPES

14 failure types. 14 different strategies.

Most processors' built-in retry uses the same fixed schedule regardless of why the payment failed. Reflow knows the difference — and acts accordingly.

decline code: insufficient_funds
Insufficient Funds
Retry on next likely payday window (Wed/Thu/Fri 9am) based on customer location and history.
82% recovery rate
decline code: expired_card
Expired Card
Send update-card email immediately. Retry within 10 minutes of customer clicking the update link.
91% recovery rate
decline code: do_not_honor
Do Not Honor
Retry once at 4h. If fails again, escalate to human review — automated recovery is rarely effective here.
31% recovery rate
decline code: card_declined
Generic Decline
Try a different time-of-day window. Send soft email nudge. If 3 retries fail, escalate.
64% recovery rate
decline code: card_not_supported
Card Not Supported
Card type incompatible. Prompt customer to add a new payment method — no retry will work.
78% recovery rate
decline code: lost_card
Lost / Stolen Card
Immediate email alert. Card is permanently invalid. Customer must add a new card to continue.
88% recovery rate
decline code: auth_required
3DS / Auth Required
Send payment authentication link. Retry immediately after customer completes 3D Secure flow.
87% recovery rate
decline code: issuer_unavailable
Issuer Not Available
Temporary bank outage. Retry after 2h, then again at 6h. Usually self-resolves without customer action.
76% recovery rate
RETRY TIMING

Why timing is the biggest variable in recovery rate

❌ Standard processor retry

Retries on Day 3, Day 5, Day 7 — fixed schedule, regardless of why the payment failed or when the customer is most likely to have funds.

fixed schedule 35–45% recovery rate

✅ Reflow's approach

For insufficient funds, we learn the customer's likely payday (Wed–Fri, 8–10am). We retry then. Not on a calendar — on behaviour.

behaviour-based 68–78% recovery rate

RECOVERY CHANNELS

Not just retries. The right message, at the right time, via the right channel.

Email Recovery

Personalised, human-sounding emails sent at the optimal time of day. Not templated spam — written for your brand and timed to the customer's behaviour.

// sample email (expired card)
Hey Rohan — your Pro plan payment on Dec 28 didn't go through. Looks like your card may have expired. Takes 30 seconds to update: [Update payment →]

SMS Nudges

For high-value failed payments, a short SMS nudge dramatically increases response rate. Open rate: 98%. Sent only when the amount justifies it and the customer has opted in.

// sample SMS
Reflow (via Stackflow): Hi Sofia, your $149 payment failed. Update your card in 30s: reflow.io/update/4f2x — Reply STOP to opt out.

In-App Nudges

A non-disruptive banner inside your product. Customers who are actively using your product when they see it convert at the highest rate of any channel.

// in-app banner (Scale plan)
⚠ Your payment didn't go through. Update your card → to keep your account active.
